    About Us

    Thousands of pets go missing every year, and countless stray animals wander the streets without anyone knowing where they belong. Behind every missing pet is an owner hoping to bring their companion home, and behind every stray is an animal that deserves safety, care, and a chance at finding a loving home. Unfortunately, getting the right information to the right people at the right time can be difficult.

    Spotter was created to bridge that gap, starting right here in San Ramon, California. We want to build a stronger, more connected community where residents can work together to find missing pets, report stray animal sightings, and help animals get the care they need. By focusing on San Ramon as our home base, we aim to connect local residents, pet owners, animal shelters, and nearby veterinary clinics so that important information can reach the people best positioned to help.

    Our goal is to make finding missing pets faster and helping stray animals easier through a community-driven platform that brings sightings and information together in one place. We believe that when neighbors work together and share what they see, even a small report can make a meaningful difference. As Spotter grows, we hope to expand beyond San Ramon and help more communities create safer environments for their animals.

    Whether you’re searching for a missing pet, have spotted an animal in need, or simply want to make a difference, you can be part of the solution. Every report, shared post, and helping hand brings us one step closer to reuniting lost pets with their families and helping stray animals find safety.

    Lost a pet?

    Losing a pet can be stressful and overwhelming, especially when you don’t know where to begin. Spotter helps you spread the word by sharing important details about your missing pet, including photos, descriptions, and the last known location. The more people who see your report, the greater the chance that someone will recognize your pet and help bring them home. We also encourage owners to contact nearby animal shelters and veterinary clinics to increase their chances of a safe reunion.

    Found a stray?

    Seeing an animal wandering alone can leave you wondering whether it has a home or needs immediate assistance. Spotter encourages community members to share sightings and relevant details so others nearby can stay informed. If it’s safe to do so, check for identification and contact a local shelter or veterinarian to see whether the animal has a microchip. By reporting what you’ve seen, you can help connect a lost animal with its owner or with someone who can provide the care it needs.

    Want to help?

    You don’t have to own a pet to make a difference. Every shared post, reported sighting, and act of kindness can help bring attention to an animal in need. You can support Spotter by spreading awareness, following our Instagram, sharing lost-pet reports, and encouraging others to get involved. Together, we can build a more connected community where people look out for animals and work toward bringing more pets safely home.

    Starting in San Ramon, we’re building a community where no pet has to stay lost longer than necessary.
