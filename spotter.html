What Spotter does

Thousands of pets go missing every year, and many strays never get seen by the people who could help. Spotter closes that gap by getting the right information to the right people quickly.

Lost a pet?

Contact nearby shelters and vets right away, and check them often. Share a clear photo and where your pet was last seen.

Found a stray?

Check for a collar or tag, then take the animal to a vet or shelter to be scanned for a microchip.

Want to help?

Donate, follow our Instagram, or share a lost-pet post. Every share puts another pair of eyes on the street.
