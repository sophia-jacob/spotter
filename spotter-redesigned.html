Start here / 01

The next right step.

Clear guidance for a stressful moment. Small actions can make a real difference.

01 — LOST A PET

Start close.
Spread the word.

Search nearby, contact local shelters and veterinary clinics, and share a recent photo with the last-seen location. Keep checking back.

Find shelters near you ↗
02 — FOUND AN ANIMAL

Keep them safe.
Find their people.

Approach carefully and never put yourself at risk. Look for a tag, report the animal to local services, and ask a vet or shelter about a microchip scan.

Find animal services ↗
03 — WANT TO HELP

Be the extra
pair of eyes.

Share verified lost-pet posts, help neighbors reach local resources, and follow community updates. Being observant counts.

The reason behind Spotter / 03

Because they
belong home.

Pets are family. And when they disappear, a little direction and a caring community can make an overwhelming day feel more manageable.

Spotter exists to make helpful information easier to find, encourage neighbors to look out for one another, and give more animals a better chance of getting home.
